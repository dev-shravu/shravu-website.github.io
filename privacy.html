Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At Secure Tunnel, privacy isn't just a feature; it is the fundamental architecture of our application. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to a Zero-Knowledge framework.

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Secure Tunnel operates on a strict Zero-Knowledge basis. Your messages, media, and call data are encrypted on your device. We do not hold the decryption keys, meaning we cannot read your messages, listen to your calls, or share your data with third parties.

2. Local Device Storage (The Vault)

Once a message is delivered to a recipient, it is permanently burned from our delivery servers and stored exclusively in a secure, encrypted SQLite vault on your local device. We do not maintain cloud backups of your conversation history.

3. Disappearing Messages & Kill Switch

  • Auto-Expiry: You can set messages to disappear automatically after a specified time limit.
  • Kill Switch: Secure Tunnel allows you to remotely delete messages from a recipient's device. When triggered, local files and media are permanently wiped from the physical device storage.
  • Auto-Deletion: For your security, any message older than 30 days is automatically purged from your local device vault.

4. Audio and Video Calls

Audio and video calls are securely routed. The application requires Camera and Microphone permissions solely to facilitate active, user-initiated communication. Call streams are not recorded, stored, or monitored by Secure Tunnel.

5. Push Notifications

To alert you of new secure connections, we use push notification tokens. Notifications contain encrypted payloads that only your local application can decipher upon waking up.

6. Changes to this Policy

As we implement new security features, this policy may be updated. Because we do not collect personal email addresses, we will notify you of any major changes directly through the Secure Tunnel app interface.
